gsc-archive

gsc-archive is a personal tool used by one person, the owner of this page, to keep a local backup of their own Google account (Gmail, Google Photos, Google Drive) on their own computer and to tidy up their own Google storage.

It is not offered to anyone else. It has no users other than the owner and no server: it runs only on the owner's computer and phone.

Google Drive access is used to copy the owner's own files to the owner's computer (read-only), to keep an encrypted backup folder of the owner's phone photos, and, only after the owner's explicit approval, to move the owner's files into folders.
